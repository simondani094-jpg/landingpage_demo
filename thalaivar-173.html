The Concept

A high-octane conceptual motion poster imagining the collaboration between Superstar Rajinikanth and director Cibi Chakaravarthi. The visual style captures a raw, intense atmosphere fitting for a massive action thriller.

The Execution

Designed a striking title typography and integrated dynamic lighting effects. The color palette revolves around aggressive reds and warm tones to signify intensity, complemented by Anirudh's musical identity.
